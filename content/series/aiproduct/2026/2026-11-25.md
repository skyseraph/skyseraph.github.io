> *"Models aren't quite as good at tasks that require judgment as they are at coding."*
> — MIT Technology Review 对 METR 报道的总结，[链接](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/20/1149399/the-most-misunderstood-graph-in-ai-just-got-a-major-update/)
